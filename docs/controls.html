How it handles

Flight assist on holds the speed you set with the throttle and keeps the ship travelling where the nose points. Off, the drives just push and you drift until you counter-thrust.

Turn tightest at lower speed. Your pilot can only take so much g, so turn rate falls as speed rises. The HUD shows the best turning speed.

Power pips. Six pips are shared between engines, shields and weapons. Pressing a system's key moves a pip to it, taking half from each of the others.

Mouse flying. The mouse moves a dot inside a ring at the centre of the screen. The further the dot from the centre, the harder you turn. Bring it back to fly straight.

Lead indicator. The red diamond near your target shows where to aim so your shots arrive where it will be.

Collisions. The station is solid: bump it gently and you bounce off, ram it and you take damage. Cruise drops you out before you reach a planet or star. Fly into one and you lose the ship.

Landing. Set a course to a surface port (amber on the system map) and cruise in. Fly down to the pad, come in below 25 m/s and press Enter. Touch down gently anywhere else and you land; come down fast and you crash.

Docking. Fly within 500 m of the station below 80 m/s, then press Enter. The game saves automatically each time you dock.

Getting around a system. Open the system map, click a planet, station or port and set a course, then press J. With a course set, cruise is an autopilot: it turns you to the target, builds speed once you're lined up, and drops you out beside it. Push the stick or mouse well over to take control; let go and it takes over again. Without a course, J is a fast drive that goes where you point.

Time. Speed time up to 20 times for long cruises and descents. It drops back to normal by itself in a fight, near a dock, low over the ground, or when you drop out of cruise.

Gravity. Worlds pull according to their mass. Flight assist holds you up like any other drift; turn it off and you fall.

Missiles. Keep your target near the nose and a ring closes in on it; when it turns solid red and reads LOCKED, fire. Fighters carry six, restocked when you dock. When one is coming for you, turn hard across its path.

Aim assist. When your target's lead point is within a few degrees of the nose, your guns swing to it.

Jobs. The job you're following shows top right with your next step. Its system is ringed and routed on the galaxy map, and its station is marked on the system map. Deliver by docking at the named station or port. L switches job; the galaxy map lists them all.

Travel. Pick a system on the map and jump. Days pass on the way, the galaxy moves on, and jobs for that system pay out on arrival. Gate systems are linked by routes. Zoom right in to see the stars between them: any star inside the green ring is in range of your jump drive.

Galaxus is a working title. Controls are for the current early build and will change.
